summary: "(_Own summary_) Using a country-sector panel, we document that China's exports to the euro area have risen recent years, and shifted from labour-intensive final goods toward advanced-manufacturing intermediates. We assess the effects in a multi-country, multi-sector DSGE model with global production networks. We find sharply asymmetric effects: cheaper intermediate inputs boost EU production and lower inflation, while competing final goods displace it, leaving the net short-run effect on euro area GDP expansionary."
